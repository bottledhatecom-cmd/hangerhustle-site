FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. Open it in your browser and start adding items. No account needed.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. If you sign in with Google, a private backup goes to the Hanger Hustle app folder in your own Google Drive. You can also export everything to CSV or JSON. See the privacy policy.

How do I install the Android app (APK)?
  1. On your Android phone, tap Android download above to get HangerHustle-1.0.1.apk.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser (for example Chrome) to install unknown apps: Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps, then turn it on for your browser.
  3. Tap Install, then open Hanger Hustle from your home screen.

A Google Play Store release is coming, so soon you'll be able to install it from there instead.

How do I install it on iPhone?

Open app.hangerhustle.com in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens full screen like any other app.

Do I need the APK?

No. You can use the web app in any phone browser and add it to your home screen from the browser menu.

Does it post to Poshmark, Mercari or Vinted for me?

No. Hanger Hustle prepares a clean listing you copy into each site. Direct posting is coming for eBay and Etsy only, through their official APIs.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Once it's loaded, you can add and edit items offline. Opening sold-listing searches needs a connection.
